Members discovered palliative and oncology care alongside experienced professionals of the Hospital of Portimão, reflecting on the medical, human and spiritual dimensions of accompanying patients at the end of life. The week also included a public session "Cuidar com fé e esperança" (Care with hope) and time to enjoy the Algarve together.

> “I leave this experience carrying transformations and improvements deep within my soul.”
