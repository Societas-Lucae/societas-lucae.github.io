The XXVII World Congress of Catholic Doctors proposed a profound reflection on the mission of the Catholic doctor in the light of the Social Doctrine of the Church, from the challenges of the Industrial Revolution to the dilemmas of the current Digital Revolution. Societas Lucae took part in the organization alongside FIAMC and the Brazilian Catholic medical associations, and hosted a session on the personal and professional growth of young Catholic physicians.
