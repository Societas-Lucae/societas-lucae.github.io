This website is a static site published by Societas Lucae. We take your privacy seriously and keep things simple.

## What we collect

- **Nothing automatically.** The site does not use cookies, trackers or analytics.
- **Membership form.** When you fill in our membership form, the data you provide (name, contact details, country, studies or specialty) is stored in Google Forms and used only to get in touch with you and to connect you with your national group and our communication channels.
- **E-mail.** If you write to us, we keep your message to answer you.

## Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may record technical data such as IP addresses in its server logs; see the [GitHub privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## Your rights

You can ask us at any time to access, correct or delete the personal data we hold about you by writing to [societas.lucae@gmail.com](mailto:societas.lucae@gmail.com).
